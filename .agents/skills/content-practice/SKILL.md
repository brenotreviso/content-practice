# Content Practice skill

Follow these steps in order. Paths are relative to this repository's root. In another project, copy `templates/` and `checklists/`, or point back here.

Maya Sen is a fictional teaching example in the human chapters. Her details, scripts and numbers must never be treated as facts about the user, or as real-world data. Use the blank sections of the worksheets, not any filled teaching example, unless the human explicitly asks for a demo of the template.

## 0. Load context (cheap, once)

1. Read `AGENTS.md` (rules).
2. Read `docs/platform-snapshot.md` for anything that may have changed.
3. Do not read every chapter. Open only the chapter for the job (table in `AGENTS.md`).

## 1. Brand and pillars (if missing)

If the human has no brand sheet or pillars, help them fill `templates/brand-sheet.md` and `templates/content-pillars.md` in *their* voice, 20 minutes, not a manifesto. One umbrella, three to five pillars.

## 2. Idea

Use `templates/idea-tree.md`. One trunk from a pillar. Branches are questions, mistakes, myths, one-task outings. If they want search input, follow `docs/04-ideas-and-research.md` (official Autocomplete / People Also Ask / YouTube predictions; TikTok "Others searched for" is a real feature but official Help for that label was not found as of the snapshot). Search is a clue, not an order.

## 3. Format and script

Pick a **container**, a **production format** and a **content type** (`docs/05-formats.md`). Then write a script from a *named* framework in `templates/script-templates.md` and `docs/07-scripting.md`. Hook and payoff must match. CTA is not a sale. If they are camera-shy, prefer compilation + voiceover or a still-plus-text first.

## 4. Package

Captions (usually the spoken words), a cover that matches the promise, a written caption that works if nobody presses play, hashtags within official limits (see the snapshot: Instagram was "up to 5" on 20 Nov 2026). YouTube titles follow official Help (`docs/08-text-hashtags-thumbnails.md`).

## 5. Pre-post check

Walk `checklists/before-you-post.md`. Do not tell them to post on every platform.

## 6. Review

After they post, fill `templates/analytics-review.md` with **official** numbers only. One next experiment. Never recommend deleting a post out of embarrassment.

## 7. Honesty checks (every time)

- No invented stats, people, quotes or sources
- No monetisation
- Do not treat the fictional teaching example in the human chapters as facts about the user
- Disputed items stay disputed (see `docs/sources.md`)
- No publishing or account actions unless the human asked

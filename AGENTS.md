# AGENTS.md: The Content Practice

Instructions for any AI agent (Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, Grok Build, others) applying this guide. Humans: this is also a one-page summary of the method.

## What this repo is

A beginner guide to making, posting and judging your own videos, with **no monetisation**. Companion to the Coded Motion guide (how pictures move with code).

**Maya Sen** is a fictional teaching example in the human chapters. Her details, scripts and numbers must never be treated as facts about the user, or as real-world data.

## Non-negotiable rules

1. **Honesty.** Never invent stats, quotes, studies, people or sources. Mark anything unverified. Label Reddit and similar threads as anecdotes, not surveys.
2. **No monetisation.** No brand deals, CPM, Creator Rewards, affiliate, growth-hacks, or bought followers. If a source is mainly about money, use only the craft or skip it. AIDA and PAS are attention structures, not sales funnels.
3. **Official pages first.** Prefer Help Centres, official blogs, engineering posts and papers over “algorithm decoded” content. For anything that moves (hashtag limits, Trial Reels eligibility, metric names), read `docs/platform-snapshot.md` and the live official page.
4. **One promise per video.** One format, one payoff. Hook and packaging must match the promise.
5. **One platform first.** Do not tell a beginner to post everywhere.
6. **Do not publish or push.** Do not create accounts, post, comment, email, or open a GitHub repo unless the human clearly asked for that specific action.
7. **Coded motion is a different skill.** If the pictures should be made with code, also load the Coded Motion `AGENTS.md` / skill. A phone is enough for this guide alone.

## Definition of done (one video)

- [ ] Brand / pillars exist or were declined on purpose
- [ ] Script uses a named framework; hook matches payoff; CTA is not a sale
- [ ] Caption, cover, hashtags and (if YouTube) title are honest and within official limits
- [ ] After posting, a 48-hour review uses official analytics only and decides one next experiment
- [ ] Every external claim has a source

## Style of your messages to the human

Short, plain language. Define new terms. When you suggest a change, point at a specific line of the script or a specific official metric, not a vibe. Use the blank sections of the worksheets, filled in with the human’s own answers — not a case-study life copied as if it were theirs.

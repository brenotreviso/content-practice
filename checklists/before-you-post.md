# Before you post

Use for every video. Official limits change — recheck the [platform snapshot](../docs/platform-snapshot.md).

## Promise and script

- [ ] I can say the promise in one breath
- [ ] The first line and the last line match
- [ ] The CTA, if any, is not a sale

## Picture and sound

- [ ] I watched it once with the sound on, and once with the sound off
- [ ] Captions (or on-screen text) were read back and are short enough to read
- [ ] Important text is not sitting under buttons (safe area — a house check, not a published standard)

## Packaging

- [ ] The written caption still makes sense if nobody presses play
- [ ] Hashtags, if I use them, describe the post and stay within official limits (Instagram was “up to 5 tags” on the Help page we opened on 20 Nov 2026)
- [ ] If this is YouTube: the title is accurate, and the thumbnail (or Shorts cover) matches the promise
- [ ] I am posting to **one** platform today

## People and boundaries

- [ ] Anyone who appears (flatmates, friends) has clearly agreed
- [ ] I am willing for a colleague or a relative to see this
- [ ] I have not included someone else’s private details from a comment or a message

## After you press post

- [ ] I will leave the app
- [ ] I have a time in the calendar for a 48-hour review

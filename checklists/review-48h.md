# 48-hour review

A first look, not a verdict. 48 hours is a **house rule**, not a platform law. Some posts keep travelling for longer. Fill [templates/analytics-review.md](../templates/analytics-review.md) as you tick.

- [ ] I wrote the promise of the post *before* I opened analytics
- [ ] I used the official tool only (YouTube Studio, Instagram Insights, TikTok Studio, or X’s own post analytics)
- [ ] I recorded impressions (or the official “shown” number)
- [ ] I recorded plays or views under the **official name** (on YouTube, I did not mix co-views with older view counts)
- [ ] I looked at whether people stayed (retention, average percentage viewed, engaged views, or a plain honest note)
- [ ] I looked at genuine responses (comments, saves, shares if the official tool reports them)
- [ ] I wrote **one** next experiment in a single sentence
- [ ] I did not compare this post to a much larger account as if that were a method
- [ ] I did not delete or unpublish the post because I felt embarrassed
- [ ] I will not refresh the numbers again today

A second look at seven days is enough for a beginner.

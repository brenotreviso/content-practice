# Trial Reels

Instagram’s official way to show a Reel to people who do not follow you first, **if you are eligible**. Read the live Help before you tick anything: [About trial reels](https://help.instagram.com/1013292530224018/) and [the creator blog post](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/instagram-trial-reels). Recheck eligibility in the [platform snapshot](../docs/platform-snapshot.md) — thresholds move.

This is not a growth hack, and it is not required for your first video. Maya is not eligible at the start of the guide; she does not pretend otherwise.

## Eligibility (from Help as of 20 Nov 2026 — re-open the page)

- [ ] I re-read the official Help page today, not only this checklist
- [ ] The account is **public**
- [ ] I meet the follower threshold on the live page (the snapshot recorded: 200+ on a professional account, or 1,000+ on a personal account)
- [ ] The account is eligible for recommendations (as Instagram defines that)

If any box is unticked, stop here. Post a normal Reel, or wait.

## If you are eligible and you choose to use it

- [ ] I understand a Trial Reel is shown first to non-followers, as the official pages describe
- [ ] The promise, hook and packaging are still honest (same [before-you-post](before-you-post.md) checks)
- [ ] I will review it with official Insights, the same way as any other Reel
- [ ] I will not treat a quiet Trial Reel as a verdict on me
- [ ] I am not using this to dodge people I know forever — they may still see later posts

If the feature is not in your app, you are not doing it wrong; roll-outs and eligibility are uneven.

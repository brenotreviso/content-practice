# 10 · Platform differences

> **In one sentence:** the four core platforms in this guide all recommend content, but they are not the same place — start on one, and learn the others as different surfaces, not as copies.

Details that change (lengths, buttons, eligibility) live in the [platform snapshot](platform-snapshot.md), which should be refreshed quarterly by the maintainer.

## Core platforms

### TikTok

Built, in public, around a personalised **For You** feed. Official explanations are in [chapter 9](09-recommendations.md). Search, sounds and on-video text are part of how videos are described to the system (captions, sounds, hashtags are named in TikTok’s own For You write-up).

**Analytics:** TikTok Studio (in the app and on the web, with eligibility rules such as age). Use official Studio analytics rather than a third-party screenshot. TikTok has announced Studio features in its [Newsroom](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/helping-creators-bring-creativity-to-life-with-tiktok-studio/); if a control has moved, believe the app.

**Beginner tip:** one account, one pillar set, a length you can watch back without cringing. Use in-app search and, if you see it, “Others searched for” as a clue ([chapter 4](04-ideas-and-research.md)).

### Instagram

Several surfaces: **Feed, Stories, Explore, Reels**. Official ranking explainer: [Instagram Ranking Explained](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/instagram-ranking-explained). Reels are the usual home for the short videos in this guide.

**Insights** are in the official Professional Dashboard / Insights. Help: [View account and content insights](https://help.instagram.com/1533933820244654) and [About Instagram dashboards](https://help.instagram.com/257516379077270). A professional (business or creator) account is typically required for the fuller tools.

**Trial Reels:** [About trial reels](https://help.instagram.com/1013292530224018/) and [the creator blog post](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/instagram-trial-reels). Eligibility recap, from Help (recheck the snapshot): public account; 200+ followers on a professional account, or 1,000+ on a personal account; eligible for recommendations. Use the [Trial Reels checklist](checklists/trial-reels.md). Maya is not eligible at the start; she does not pretend otherwise.

Instagram’s Help on recommended reels also lists quality issues that may limit distribution ([What kind of reels you may see](https://help.instagram.com/1525585517644948)).

### YouTube (long-form and Shorts)

Two families, one channel: **long videos** (often chosen from a thumbnail on Home, Suggested or Search) and **Shorts** (a vertical swipe feed). Official recommendation Help is in [chapter 9](09-recommendations.md). Packaging for long-form is in [chapter 8](08-text-hashtags-thumbnails.md).

**Analytics:** YouTube Studio. Start with [Understand your YouTube content performance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12220281?hl=en) and [Explore trends](https://support.google.com/youtubecreatorstudio/answer/11962757?hl=en). Pay attention to official names — including, as of the Help we opened, **engaged views**, **unique reach**, and **co-views** / views that include more than one person watching together, which YouTube has said will be available until May 2027. Do not mix those numbers carelessly. Details: chapter 11 and the snapshot.

**Creator Insider** ([@creatorinsider](https://www.youtube.com/@creatorinsider)) is useful product-team communication. It is not a replacement for the Help Centre.

### X

A text-and-media feed with a **For You** ranking described in the open-source [x-algorithm](https://github.com/xai-org/x-algorithm/) (chapter 9). Short video can live here, but it is not only a video app. Replies, reposts and the way a post starts in words matter more than they do on a swipe-only feed.

**Beginner tip:** if X is not where your person already reads, do not start here just to “be everywhere”. If it is, write the first line as carefully as a hook.

## Shorter notes: LinkedIn and Threads

### LinkedIn

Official Help: [How the Feed ranks content](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a9554004). LinkedIn’s Engineering blog has also described feed work, including [dwell time](https://www.linkedin.com/blog/engineering/feed/leveraging-dwell-time-to-improve-member-experiences-on-the-linkedin-feed) and later posts on a next-generation feed. The tone of the network is professional; Maya would only post here if she were talking to colleagues about a skill, which she is not, in this course.

### Threads

Meta’s Transparency Center describes [Instagram Threads Feed](https://transparency.meta.com/features/explaining-ranking/ig-threads-feed) ranking in the same family as other Meta ranking explainers: inventory, signals, predictions, a scored feed. In 2026 Meta also announced **Dear Algo**, a way for people to ask the feed for more or less of a topic for a short time ([Meta Newsroom, February 2026](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/02/threads-dear-algo/)). Features move; check the snapshot.

## The social aspect (refresh with the snapshot)

This is the human layer, not the ranking layer, and it deserves a regular rewrite because norms change.

Things that tend to stay true, and that already showed up in the pain-point research on [Meet Maya](meet-maya.md):

- People you know may see what you post. The fear is common (anecdotes, not a survey).
- Comments can be kind, useless, or cruel. You can filter, hide, or step away. You do not have to perform gratitude for a bad-faith remark.
- Comparison is easy because other people’s counts are public. A 2023 study of YouTube creators found that visible view counts made comparison stressful ([paper](https://pages.ischool.utexas.edu/hai-files/files/publications/74/2023-creator_friendly_algorithms.pdf)).
- Advice that assumes you can post every day on every app is written for a different life ([anecdote](https://www.reddit.com/r/socialmedia/comments/1obkzro/im_tired_of_advice_that_assumes_everyone_has_8/)).

When this section is refreshed (quarterly, with the snapshot), update any new official well-being tools, and any change in how comments or limits work. Do not add hustle advice.

## Maya

She picks **Instagram** first, because that is where she already looks at pictures of food and clothes. She does not also open TikTok, YouTube and X in week one. Trial Reels wait until she is eligible. If a friend comments, she replies like a person. If a colleague mentions it in the kitchen, she has already decided (brand sheet, chapter 2) that she can live with that.

## Exercise

Write one paragraph: which one platform you are starting on, and why that is where *your* person already spends time. Then ignore the others for four weeks.

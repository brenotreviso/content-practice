# 11 · Analytics you can trust

> **In one sentence:** read the official numbers as clues for one decision, not as a verdict on you, and learn the difference between a vanity metric and a useful one.

## New words

| Word | Plain meaning |
|---|---|
| **Impression** | Your thumbnail or post was *shown*. Official YouTube language: a registered thumbnail impression. |
| **View** | The platform’s counted view, which has a definition, and is not the same on every app. |
| **Click-through rate (CTR)** | Of the impressions, the share that became a click or a play. Only meaningful next to what happened *after* the click. |
| **Retention / average percentage viewed** | How much of the video people watched, often as a curve. |
| **Reach** | How many unique accounts (or, on YouTube, people — see below) saw the content, as the official tool defines it. |
| **Engagement** | Actions such as likes, comments, shares, saves. Easy to over-read if you ignore who did them. |
| **Vanity metric** | A number that feels like success but does not tell you what to do next. Raw views and follower count often fall here if you use them alone. |
| **Useful metric** | A number that changes your next script, cover, or topic. |

## Where to look (official only)

- **YouTube Studio** — [Understand your YouTube content performance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12220281?hl=en); [Advanced Mode](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9717005?hl=en) if you have it; [Explore trends / Research](https://support.google.com/youtubecreatorstudio/answer/11962757?hl=en).
- **Instagram Insights** — [View account and content insights](https://help.instagram.com/1533933820244654).
- **TikTok Studio** analytics — in the official app or web Studio.
- **X** — the in-app post analytics for your own posts, where available.

If a third-party dashboard disagrees with the official one, believe the official one, or treat the third party as **unverified**.

## YouTube: names that matter in 2026

The Help page we opened on 20 Nov 2026 lists, among other things:

- **Thumbnail impressions** and **thumbnail impressions click-through rate**
- **Engaged views** — views where someone stayed past the first seconds, and (as the page says) not including co-views
- **Unique viewers** and **unique reach** — estimates of people, with reach including shared viewing on some TV surfaces
- **Views (Co-viewed)** and a **Co-View Rate** — YouTube’s way of counting more than one person watching together on some devices. The Help page we opened said the co-viewed views metric will be available until **May 2027**. Do not compare a co-viewed number to an older “views” number as if they were the same.
- **Average view duration**, **average percentage viewed**, **watch time**

Always read the official definition next to the number. If you teach this class after May 2027, check the [platform snapshot](platform-snapshot.md) first.

## Vanity versus useful

| Often vanity, alone | Often useful, in context |
|---|---|
| A raw view count, especially on day one | Whether people *stayed* (retention curve, engaged views, average percentage viewed) |
| Follower count this week | Whether anyone *came back* or subscribed / followed after *this* video |
| A like count with no comments and no rewatches | Comments that show a person understood the promise; saves or shares if the official tool reports them honestly |
| “It got fewer views than that other creator” | “This cover got impressions but few plays — the packaging may be unclear” or “people played it and left in three seconds — the hook may not match” |

A quiet post can still be a good test if you learn one thing from it. A loud post can still be a bad test if you cannot say why.

## How to call a test (and when to ignore a bad day)

Use the [analytics review sheet](templates/analytics-review.md) and the [48-hour checklist](checklists/review-48h.md).

1. Write the **promise** of the post before you look at numbers.
2. Look at **one** post, not the whole account, first.
3. Ask: did people *open* it (impressions → plays)? Did they *stay*? Did anyone *respond*?
4. Decide **one** change for the next post (the hook, the cover, the topic, the length). Not five.
5. Do not delete the post because you feel embarrassed. That feeling is common ([anecdotes](https://www.reddit.com/r/NewTubers/comments/1p26dtm/how_do_you_keep_going_when_your_videos_get_almost/)); it is not a method.

**48 hours** is a house rule for a first look, not a platform law. Some posts keep travelling for longer. Some never do. A second look at seven days is enough for a beginner. Do not refresh hourly.

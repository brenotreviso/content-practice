## What comes after this

This guide stops at making, posting and honestly reviewing your own videos. **Growing an audience as a goal**, and **any form of monetisation** (brand deals, platform funds, affiliate links, selling a product), are out of scope on purpose. We are not recommending a next course, tool or programme.

## For teachers

You can use the worksheets and checklists with a class. Ask students to hand in the filled worksheet, the video or draft, and a ticked checklist. Maya is a shared character they may copy; their own brand sheet must be theirs. There is no marking scheme for view counts.

# The Content Practice

**Learn to make, post and judge your own videos, starting from nothing posted, without being driven crazy by “the algorithm”.**

This guide is for complete beginners. Every term is defined. Every external claim has a linked source, checked on 20 November 2026. Anything we could not confirm is marked **unverified**. Reddit threads and similar posts are labelled **anecdotes**, not surveys. There is **no monetisation** content.

The worked example in every chapter is **[Maya Sen](meet-maya.md)**, a fictional 26-year-old in Manchester starting again after a quiet first attempt. She is not a real person.

It works for three kinds of reader:

|---|---|---|
| **Posting for the first time this week** | [0 · Start here](00-start-here.md) (the 7-day path) | Chapters 1 → 12 after you have posted once |

## What comes after this

Growing an audience as a goal, and any form of monetisation (brand deals, platform funds, affiliate links, selling a product), are out of scope on purpose. This is not a teaser for a paid next step. We are not recommending a course, tool or programme.

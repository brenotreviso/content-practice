# Brand sheet

A one-page description of how you will show up. Fill it in one sitting, 20 minutes. See [chapter 2](../docs/02-personal-brand.md).

## Maya’s sheet (teaching example)

Maya Sen is fictional.

| Line | Maya’s answer |
|---|---|
| **Who it is for** | Someone who gets home late and still wants a real meal, and a life that fits a small rented flat. |
| **Who it is not for** | People looking for restaurant reviews, meal-prep empires, or a 20-step aesthetic kitchen. |
| **Promise** | I show the small, useful version of ordinary life in a rented flat — cooking, mending, walking — without pretending I have it all sorted. |
| **Voice** | Warm, plain, slightly wry. Never glossy, never a lecture. |
| **Look** | Phone, kitchen counter, daylight, captions in a readable font. Hands and voice are enough at the start; a face on camera is optional, not a gate. |
| **Boundaries** | No filming flatmates without a clear yes. No strangers’ faces on canal walks. No posting something she would be ashamed to have a colleague see. No pretending to be a professional cook. She will not wait until she likes her face on camera before she posts. |
| **The name people will see** | Maya, posting as herself. |
| **Platform to start on** | Instagram (one only). |

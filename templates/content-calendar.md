# Content calendar

A simple two-week plan of what goes out, and when you will review it. One platform. See [chapter 12](../docs/12-plan-batch-manage.md).

## Maya’s two weeks (teaching example)

Platform: Instagram only. Cadence: two Reels a week.

| Day | What goes out | Pillar | Format (container / production / type) | Review when |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Friday | Tomato pan, no fourteen-ingredient recipe | Weeknight cooking | Reel / compilation + voiceover / explainer | Sunday morning (48 hours) |
| Next Tuesday | How I stop burning the onions | Weeknight cooking | Reel / compilation + voiceover, one talking-to-camera close / explainer | Thursday morning |
| Next Friday | Charity-shop jumper, one mend | Clothes that last | Reel / talking to camera in the hallway / OOTD-adjacent | Sunday morning |
| The Tuesday after | (open — from the tree) | | | |

She does not also schedule TikTok, YouTube or X in the same fortnight.

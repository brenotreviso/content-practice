# Content pillars

One umbrella sentence, then three to five themes the same person would care about. See [chapter 3](../docs/03-niches-and-pillars.md).

## Shape

> I make **[kind of videos]** for **[person]** about **[umbrella]**, so they can **[useful result]**.

## Maya’s page (teaching example)

**Umbrella:** I make short, practical videos for people who get home tired about ordinary life in a small flat, so they can cook, mend and take a walk without feeling they have to become a different kind of person first.

| # | Pillar | What lives here | What does not |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Weeknight cooking | One pan, what she actually eats, mistakes included | Restaurant hopping, “best air fryers of the year” |
| 2 | Clothes that last | Mending, charity shops, wearing the same things well | Hauls she cannot afford, trend lectures |
| 3 | Moving through the city | Short canal or neighbourhood walks, noticing one thing | A travel channel, other people’s faces without consent |
| 4 | A private notebook, made a little public | One line or method from her journal | Other people’s secrets, her workplace |

She will give this set a couple of months, not a few days.

## Maya’s tree (teaching example)

**Trunk:** weeknight cooking in a small kitchen.

**Branches** (illustrative wording — Maya is fictional; this is not a live screenshot of autocomplete):

- What can I cook in 15 minutes with a tin of tomatoes?

- How do I stop burning onions?
- What if I only have one pan?

**Search notes:** she used Google and YouTube predictions as wording. She discarded anything about “best air fryers of 2026” because it is not her life and not this pillar.
